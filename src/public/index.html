End-to-End Architecture

From invoice arrival to business decision.

Vendor invoices arrive across disconnected formats. The platform unifies them into a continuous verification pipeline before financial commitment.

Channel 01

Physical Invoice

Paper documents received at site gates, mailrooms, or facilities. Digitized through optical character recognition and Document Information Extraction.

  • Scanned PDF / TIFF optical intake
  • Header and line item geometry parsing
Channel 02

Email Inbound

Electronic invoices submitted by suppliers directly into corporate accounts payable mailboxes. Automatically ingested with attachment validation.

  • Sender domain and SPF / DKIM verification
  • Duplicate hash check across prior runs
Channel 03

E-Invoice

Government Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) payloads and PEPPOL network exchanges. Direct statutory JSON integration with digital signature verification.

  • 64-character IRN cryptographic verification
  • Direct GST portal GSP sync readiness
Unified Invoice Workflow

All intake channels converge into a canonical enterprise invoice data model with normalized supplier references, line item descriptions, tax structures, and currency conversions.

Stage 01

PO / GR Validation

Automated 3-way matching cross-referencing invoice line items with SAP Purchase Orders (EKKO/EKPO) and warehouse Goods Receipts (MSEG).

Stage 02

Business Owner Decision

Invoices with commercial variances or Non-PO classifications are routed to the responsible business owner for formal approval or dispute.

Stage 03

Exception Handling

Dedicated isolation for price variances, over-deliveries, vendor master mismatches, duplicate documents, and quality blocks.

Stage 04

Finance Processing

Clean Core execution in SAP S/4HANA: touchless invoices post directly via MIRO, while exceptions stage as parked records via MIR7.

Stage 05

GST Reconciliation

Statutory auto-matching against government GSTR-2B statements ensuring valid Input Tax Credit (ITC) eligibility and preventing tax leakages.

Human-in-the-Loop Governance

The business owner stays in control.

Invoices requiring business validation are routed to the person responsible for the underlying business requirement.

The system brings together evidence, automated three-way reconciliations, and cognitive recommendations, while the authorized business owner retains the final authority to determine commercial action.
Option 01

Accept

Approves the commercial charge and confirms goods or services rendered despite variance. Triggers auto-proceed re-evaluation and clears the invoice for financial posting.

Option 02

Reject

Disallows the invoice completely. Halts accounts payable settlement, records the reason in the enterprise audit trail, and prepares a formal vendor rejection advisory.

Option 03

Send Back

Returns the invoice to the vendor or procurement team requesting a corrected invoice or credit note, ensuring erroneous liabilities do not enter S/4HANA.

Option 04

Request Clarification

Opens an audit-tracked query with the warehouse receiver, buyer, or supplier contact while keeping the invoice staged safely without missing SLA windows.

Risk & Anomaly Controls

Active Exception Management

Early detection and structured classification of procurement, logistical, and statutory anomalies.

PO Mismatch Validation

Discrepancy between invoice reference and open SAP purchase orders, or missing line item correspondence.

Quantity Variance Tolerance

Invoiced count exceeds goods receipt quantities or breaches contractual over-delivery tolerance limits.

Amount Variance Price Key PP

Net unit price exceeds PO agreed price baseline beyond configured SAP Tolerance Key PP thresholds.

Duplicate Invoice Fraud Risk

Identical invoice number, supplier code, or cryptographic content hash detected across current and historical runs.

Missing PO Non-PO

Commercial invoice without purchasing order, requiring direct cost center allocation and requisitioner confirmation.

Vendor Mismatch Security

Remit-to supplier entity or banking details diverge from the vendor master authorized on the purchase order.

GST Mismatch Tax Risk

Tax rate, HSN classification, or tax amount differs from government portal GSTR-2B statement records.

Quality Issue SAP QM

SAP Quality Management inspection lots (QALS) report defective or rejected units, blocking invoice payment.

Acceptance SLA Approaching SLA Alert

Statutory payment deadline, prompt-payment cash discount window, or internal turnaround SLA nearing breach.

Governance & Compliance

Every decision remains traceable.

Built for internal audit compliance, statutory inspections, and corporate governance.

Evidence

Exact document OCR crops, matched PO lines, and warehouse receipt vouchers preserved as immutable artifacts.

Decision

Clear deterministic categorization: AUTO_PROCEED, MANUAL_REVIEW, HOLD, or REJECT with score metrics.

User

Approver identity, departmental role, cost center authorization, and corporate email authentication recorded.

Timestamp

ISO 8601 audit-grade timestamps capturing ingestion, evaluation, validation, and posting milestones.

Reason

Explainable human-readable justification detailing why specific tolerances or policies triggered.

State Transition

Lifecycle state tracing from INGESTED through UNDER_REVIEW, BUSINESS_VALIDATED, to POSTED or PARKED.
